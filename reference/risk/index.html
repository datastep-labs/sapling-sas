    %risk¶

    Returns the risk difference, relative risk or odds ratio of pairs of groups, with its confidence interval and p-value.

    Procedure: PROC FREQ (RISKDIFF, RELRISK, CHISQ; COMMONRISKDIFF, CMH) · Family: Compare groups with a model

    Syntax¶

    %risk(
     data    =
   , where   =
   , var     =
   , event   = "Y"
   , by      =
   , within  =
   , strata  =
   , pairs   = all
   , order   =
   , measure = rd
   , ci      = wald
   , level   = 0.95
   , stats   =
   , label   =
   , out     =
   , debug   = no
 )

    Parameters¶

    data=
    The input data set, with USUBJID: one record per subject, or per subject and term with within=. (required)
    where=
    The records to use: a condition in parentheses, such as where = (saffl = 'Y'). (optional)
    var=
    The response: one variable with two values, numeric or character. (required)
    event=
    The value of var= that is the event, in quotes (event = "DISCONTINUED"). (optional, default "Y")
    by=
    The groups: one character variable. (required)
    within=
    One comparison in each group of these variables, separated by blanks, such as each term of an adverse event table with the flags of %termflags (within = aebodsys aedecod). (optional)
    strata=
    The strata of the Mantel-Haenszel risk difference: variables separated by blanks. Only with measure=rd. (optional)
    pairs=
    The pairs of groups to compare. all: each later group against each earlier one, in the order of order= (or of the values). Or the pairs separated by #, each the group first and then the group it is compared with, a value with a blank or a hyphen in quotes: pairs = "Drug" "Placebo" # "High" "Low". (optional, default all)
    order=
    The order of the groups: one numeric variable with one value per group (order = trt01an). (optional)
    measure=
    The estimate of the group against the group it is compared with: rd, the risk difference, a difference of two proportions between -1 and 1 (0.25 is 25 percentage points), not of percents; rr, the relative risk, the ratio of the two risks; or or, the odds ratio, the ratio of the two odds. A ratio has no unit. (optional, default rd) Accepts: rd, rr, or.
    ci=
    The interval of a risk difference: wald, the Wald interval (CL=WALD); newcombe, the hybrid score interval of Newcombe, from the Wilson interval of each risk (CL=NEWCOMBE); mn or miettinen-nurminen, the score interval of Miettinen and Nurminen (CL=MN); ac or agresti-caffo, the interval of Agresti and Caffo, a Wald interval with one event and one non-event added to each group (CL=AC); or mh, the interval of Mantel and Haenszel, which needs strata=. With strata= the interval is always mh, whatever ci= says. (optional, default wald)
    level=
    The confidence level of the interval: a number between 0 and 1. (optional, default 0.95)
    stats=
    The statistics, in the order of the rows: each name with the pattern of its printed number, separated by blanks. When not given: rd="X.XXX" lcl="X.XXX" ucl="X.XXX" pval="X.XXX"; for measure=rr or or, rr="XX.XX" or or="XX.XX" with lcl="XX.XX" ucl="XX.XX" pval="X.XXX". A name without a pattern takes its default pattern: see the table of default patterns. (optional)
    label=
    The label of the block in the table: a text in quotes. A block is the rows of one call in the table. (optional)
    out=
    The name of the result data set. (required)
    debug=
    yes: keep the work data sets risk. (optional, default no)* Accepts: yes, no.

    Result¶

    The data set of out=: the result shape, one row per pair and statistic: the group in the by= variable, the group it is compared with in VERSUS, and the variables of within=. The risk of a group is its subjects with the event of event= over its subjects in the records used. With measure=rd, rd is the risk of the group minus the risk of VERSUS, a difference of two proportions (0.25: 25 percentage points more in the group); with strata=, the Mantel-Haenszel estimate of that difference. With measure=rr, rr is the risk of the group over the risk of VERSUS; with measure=or, or is the odds of the event in the group over the odds in VERSUS. lcl and ucl are the limits of the estimate, pval its p-value. With pairs=all the group is the later one in the order of order=. GROUPS is the by= variable, VERSUS and the within= variables.

    Details¶

    Use %logistic for an odds ratio adjusted for covariates in a model, and %fisher for only the p-value of an adverse event. For each pair the macro makes the 2 x 2 table of the two groups (the group first) by the event of event= against the rest, one record per subject, with PROC FREQ. measure= gives the estimate:

    • rd: the risk difference, with the interval of ci=
    • rr: the relative risk, with the Wald interval of its logarithm
    • or: the odds ratio, with the Wald interval of its logarithm

    The p-value is that of Pearson's chi-square test without continuity correction. With strata= the macro gives the Mantel-Haenszel risk difference (COMMONRISKDIFF, CL=MH) and the p-value of the CMH test.

    Examples¶

    The examples run on the example data after examples/setup.sas. The output under each call is the output of that run. They also read ae_flags (made by the example of %termflags). All examples of the reference run as one program: examples/reference.sas.

     data      = adae
   , denom     = adsl
   , where     = (trtemfl = 'Y')
   , terms     = aebodsys aedecod
   , by        = trt01a
   , threshold = 5
   , out       = ae_flags
 )

    Messages¶

    A wrong call writes the message and its fix to the log and stops. The macro also gives the messages every macro shares.

    Message Fix
    ci=<ci> is not an interval here give ci= wald, newcombe, miettinen-nurminen, agresti-caffo, mh.
    ci=mh is the interval of strata= (Mantel and Haenszel) give strata=, or give ci= wald, newcombe, mn or ac.
    strata=<strata> is taken by the risk difference only give measure=rd, or no strata=.
    pairs= names the group "<group>", and no record has it. give pairs= the values of <by> as the data have them.

    See also¶

    Compare groups with a model: %lsmeans, %mmrm, %logistic, %rateratio.

    Named on this page: %termflags, %fisher.

    Proof¶

    Checked against its known answers: the risk difference, the relative risk, the odds ratio, and the risk difference of each term of an adverse event table (on a small data set, the numbers worked out independently in R); ran on SAS 9.4 M9, 2026-11-12.

    The known answers and test cases are kept with the development of the library, not in this repository.

    The examples of this page ran on SAS 9.4 M9 on 2026-11-27, with a log free of ERROR and WARNING.
