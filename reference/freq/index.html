    %freq¶

    Counts the subjects in each category and group, with their percent of the subjects of the group.

    Procedure: PROC FREQ · Family: Describe the data

    Syntax¶

    %freq(
     data      =
   , denom     =
   , where     =
   , var       =
   , by        =
   , keep      =
   , levels    =
   , total     = no
   , threshold =
   , order     =
   , orderof   =
   , orderby   =
   , stats     = n="XX" pct="(XX.X%)"
   , label     =
   , out       =
   , debug     = no
 )

    Parameters¶

    data=
    The input data set: the records to count, with USUBJID. (required)
    denom=
    The subjects of each group: the N of the percent and the columns. A data set with one row per subject and the variables of by=; data set options are allowed: adsl(where=(saffl = 'Y')). When not given, the subjects of data= (without where=). (optional)
    where=
    The records to count: a condition in parentheses, such as where = (trtemfl = 'Y'). (optional)
    var=
    The categories: one variable or several, separated by blanks, nested in their order (var = aebodsys aedecod: a term within its class). When not given, the macro counts the subjects with any record. (optional)
    by=
    The groups: one variable or several, separated by blanks. The first variable gives the columns of the table; the others give its rows or its pages (by = trt01a sex, with page= of %layout). (required)
    keep=
    Other variables to keep with the categories for their names and order, separated by blanks. PARAM and PARAMN go with PARAMCD, AVISITN with AVISIT, any <V>N with V. Each gets the value of the first record of its category. (optional)
    levels=
    The planned categories of some variables of var=: each variable followed by its values in quotes (levels = bnrind 'LOW' 'NORMAL' 'HIGH'). A planned category shows also without a subject; a record of another category is not counted. The rows of a planned variable V follow the order given, through its twin VN (1, 2 ...) in out=, unless keep= has VN. The categories of the other variables come from the records counted. (optional)
    total=
    yes: add the group "Total", all groups of the first BY variable together. A text in quotes gives the Total another name ("All subjects"). The first BY variable must be character. (optional, default no)
    threshold=
    A percent (threshold = 5): keep only the categories with this percent of the subjects or more in at least one group (the Total counts as a group). The macro compares the percent itself, not its rounded text. (optional)
    order=
    frequency: order the rows of orderof= by their subjects, the most first, and equal counts by their value. The rank goes into the twin <V>N, and %print orders the rows by it. When not given, the rows are in the order of the values. (optional)
    orderof=
    The variable of var= whose rows order=frequency ranks: one variable. When not given, the last variable of var=. (optional)
    orderby=
    The group whose subjects rank the rows for order=frequency: a value of the first BY variable, in quotes (orderby = "Placebo"). When not given, all groups together, without the Total. (optional)
    stats=
    The statistics, in the order of the rows: each name with its pattern of the printed number, separated by blanks. The names are n, pct and events. A name without a pattern takes its default pattern: see the table of default patterns. (optional, default n="XX" pct="(XX.X%)")
    label=
    The label of the block in the table: a text in quotes. A block is the rows of one call in the table. (optional)
    out=
    The name of the result data set. (required)
    debug=
    yes: keep the work data sets freq. (optional, default no)* Accepts: yes, no.

    Result¶

    The data set of out=: the result shape, one row per group, category and statistic: the variables of by= and var=, the variables of keep=, GROUPS, BLOCK, STAT, VALUE and TEXT. Every group of denom= has every category: n = 0 when no subject has it. With levels=, the twin <V>N of each planned variable gives its place in levels=; with order=frequency, the twin of orderof= gives the rank.

    Details¶

    Use %proportion when the percent needs a confidence interval, and %worst for the worst grade of each subject. A subject is counted once in each cell. A record with a missing group or category is not counted. The percent is of the subjects of the group in denom=. Without var=, the macro counts the subjects with any record. The name events in stats= adds the number of records of each cell.

    The examples run on the example data after examples/setup.sas. The output under each call is the output of that run. All examples of the reference run as one program: examples/reference.sas.

    Messages¶

    A wrong call writes the message and its fix to the log and stops. The macro also gives the messages every macro shares.

    Message Fix
    total= needs a character group first in by= (<first> is numeric) give by= a character group first (TRT01A), or total=no.
    levels= names the variable <w>, and var=<var> does not have it. give levels= variables of var= only, or add <w> to var=.

    The macro also writes these NOTE lines to the log, without stopping:

    • NOTE: [freq] No data qualified: no record meets where= and the conditions of the macro.

    Checked against its known answers: the counts and the percents of the subjects (on a small data set, the numbers worked out independently in R); ran on SAS 9.4 M9, 2026-11-12.

    The known answers and test cases are kept with the development of the library, not in this repository.

    The examples of this page ran on SAS 9.4 M9 on 2026-11-27, with a log free of ERROR and WARNING.

    Used in the proof against pharmaverse: t14_2_01 (R Submission Pilot 1), adverse_events (pharmaverse examples), demographic (pharmaverse examples). See what the proof shows.
