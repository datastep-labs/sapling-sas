    %table_model¶

    Makes a table of a continuous endpoint in one call: descriptive statistics, and the LS means of a model.

    Procedure: none of its own (those of %describe, %lsmeans, %mmrm, %table) · Family: Tables in one call

    Syntax¶

    %table_model(
     data     =
   , adsl     =
   , pop      =
   , where    =
   , var      = CHG
   , covars   =
   , visit    = AVISIT
   , by       =
   , at       =
   , order    =
   , describe =
   , method   = ancova
   , model    =
   , type     = un
   , fallback =
   , pairs    =
   , names    =
   , format   =
   , name     =
   , show     = no
   , stats    =
   , title    =
   , footnote =
   , file     =
   , debug    = no
 )

    Parameters¶

    data=
    The input data set: the records, with USUBJID. (required)
    adsl=
    The subjects: a data set with one row per subject. It gives the population and the N of each column, and its variables are merged onto the records by USUBJID. When not given, the N of a column is the subjects of its records. (optional)
    pop=
    The population: a condition on adsl= in parentheses, such as pop = (saffl = 'Y'). It is used only with adsl=. (optional)
    where=
    The records to use: a condition in parentheses, such as where = (paramcd = 'SYSBP'). (optional)
    var=
    The response: one numeric variable. (optional, default CHG)
    covars=
    The covariates of the model, separated by blanks, such as covars = base. (optional)
    visit=
    The variable of the visit: one variable. (optional, default AVISIT)
    by=
    The groups of the columns: one character variable, such as by = trt01a. (required)
    at=
    The visit of the table: a value of visit= in quotes, such as at = "Week 24". It selects the records of the descriptive blocks and of the ANCOVA, and the visit of the LS means of the MMRM. When not given, the ANCOVA uses all records. (optional)
    order=
    The numeric twin of by=, such as order = trt01an: it gives the order of the columns and of the groups in the comparisons. (optional)
    describe=
    The variables with a descriptive block before the model, separated by blanks, such as describe = base aval chg. (optional)
    method=
    ancova (%lsmeans) or mmrm (%mmrm, which needs at=). (optional, default ancova) Accepts: ancova, mmrm.
    model=
    The fixed effects of the MMRM, separated by blanks. When not given: the covariates, the by= variable, the visit= variable and the interaction of the two (such as trt01pavisit). This default has no interaction of a covariate with the visit, such as baseavisit: give model= to add one. (optional)
    type=
    The covariance structure of the MMRM: TYPE= of the REPEATED statement, such as un, cs, ar(1) or toep (type= of %mmrm). (optional, default un)
    fallback=
    Other covariance structures of the MMRM to try in their order when one does not converge, separated by blanks, such as fallback = toep cs. (optional)
    pairs=
    The pairs of groups to compare, separated by #: each group in quotes, then the group it is compared with ("Xanomeline High Dose" "Placebo"). When not given, each later group is compared with each earlier one. (optional)
    names=
    The labels of the blocks: pairs variable="text", separated by blanks, such as names = chg="Change from baseline". A block without a pair has the label of its variable, or its name. (optional)
    format=
    The labels of the categories: pairs of a variable and a format, as in a FORMAT statement (format = sex $sexf. race $racef.), as format= of %layout. (optional)
    name=
    The name of the table: the first part of the names of its data sets, such as name = t14_3_01. When not given, the last part of file= is used; without file=, name= is required. (optional)
    show=
    yes: write the long program to the log. A file path, without quotes: write the long program to that file. In both cases the macro runs it. (optional, default no)
    stats=
    The statistics of the model block, each name with its pattern. When not given: the LS means with their SE, the difference with its SE and limits, and the p-value. (optional)
    title=
    The titles of the table: one text in quotes per line (TITLE1, TITLE2 ...). They hold for this table only. When not given, the TITLE statements in force in the program are used. (optional)
    footnote=
    The footnotes of the table: one text in quotes per line (FOOTNOTE1, FOOTNOTE2 ...). They hold for this table only. When not given, the FOOTNOTE statements in force in the program are used. (optional)
    file=
    The output file without its extension: the macro writes <file>.rtf, <file>.lst and the numbers file. When not given, nothing is printed. (optional)
    debug=
    yes: keep the work data sets of the macros that the long program calls. (optional, default no) Accepts: yes, no.

    Result¶

    The data sets: <name>_<variable> (the descriptive results), <name>_ancova or <name>_mmrm (the result of the model), <name> (the stacked results), <name>_cols, <name>_display, <name>_at (with at=), <name>_pop (with adsl=) and <name>_rec (with adsl= or where=). The files: <file>.rtf, <file>.lst and the numbers file (only with file=). The global macro variable: <name>_print. With show=: the long program, in the log or in a file. In the printed table, a comparison is in the column of the group, on the rows under "vs" and the group it is compared with: the LS mean of the column group minus the LS mean of the group on the row.

    Details¶

    Use %table_visit for the statistics at every visit without a model; to add or change a block, start from its long program (show=). A block is the rows of one call in the table. The table has these blocks:

    • one block per variable of describe=: its descriptive statistics on the records of the visit of at= (%describe)
    • with method=ancova: the LS means and the differences of pairs of groups from an ANCOVA on the records of the visit of at= (%lsmeans), in a block with the label "ANCOVA, adjusted for" and the names of covars= in lower case, such as "ANCOVA, adjusted for base" (without covars=: "Analysis of variance")
    • with method=mmrm: the same from a mixed model for repeated measures over all visits, at the visit of at= (%mmrm), in a block with the label "MMRM: " and the visit, such as "MMRM: Week 24"

    With method=ancova, the records of the visit of at= must be one record per subject: give where= the parameter and the analysis flag, as %lsmeans uses the first record of a subject (with a NOTE when it leaves one out). The label of the model block is not changed by names=: change its row in <name>_display and print again, or start from the long program (show=). The table has no Total column. The long program is the program the macro writes and runs: the population, the records, one call per block, %stack and %table, the calls a person would write by hand. The macro computes nothing itself. These data sets stay in WORK, for a change and a second print:

    • <name>_<variable> and <name>_ancova or <name>_mmrm: the results, one row per number (the result shape)
    • <name>: the stacked results
    • <name>_cols: the columns (%columns)
    • <name>_display: the display data set, one row per printed line (%layout)

    The global macro variable <name>_print keeps the call of %print: change <name>_display, then print again with %print(&<name>_print.).

    Examples¶

    The examples run on the example data after examples/setup.sas. The output under each call is the output of that run. All examples of the reference run as one program: examples/reference.sas.

    Makes the ANCOVA table at Week 12: three descriptive blocks, then the LS means and difference.

    %table_model(
     data     = advs
   , adsl     = adsl
   , where    = (paramcd = 'SYSBP' AND anl01fl = 'Y' AND avisitn < 99)
   , covars   = base
   , by       = trt01p
   , at       = "Week 12"
   , order    = trt01pn
   , describe = base aval chg
   , pairs    = "Xanomeline High Dose" "Placebo"
   , title    = "Table 14.3.1 ANCOVA of the change of systolic blood pressure at Week 12"
   , file     = &spl_out./tlf/t14_3_1
 )

    Uses method=mmrm with the model of the mmrm example: the same differences at Week 24.

    %table_model(
     data     = advs
   , adsl     = adsl
   , where    = (paramcd = 'SYSBP' AND anl01fl = 'Y' AND avisitn IN (4 8 12 16 24))
   , covars   = base
   , visit    = avisit
   , by       = trt01p
   , at       = "Week 24"
   , order    = trt01pn
   , method   = mmrm
   , model    = base trt01p avisit trt01p*avisit base*avisit
   , type     = un
   , fallback = toep
   , title    = "Table 14.3.3 MMRM of the change of systolic blood pressure at Week 24"
   , file     = &spl_out./tlf/t14_3_3
 )

    Messages¶

    A wrong call writes the message and its fix to the log and stops. The macro also gives the messages every macro shares.

    Message Fix
    name= is required when file= is blank. give name=<the name of the table>, as t14_5_2.
    adsl=<ds> does not exist. give adsl= the data set of the subjects.
